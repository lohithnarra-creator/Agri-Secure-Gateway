Why AES-GCM for Agri-Firmware?

Traditional ciphers like CBC only provide confidentiality, meaning an active adversary could alter encrypted blocks without detection.

Authenticated Encryption (AEAD):
  • Confidentiality: 256-bit key ensures eavesdroppers cannot extract sensor calibrations or algorithms.
  • Authentication Tag: 128-bit MAC tag is computed over the ciphertext and nonce.
  • Tamper Proof: If an adversary flips even 1 bit in the ciphertext or tag, the mathematical validation fails and decryption is rejected!
Key Storage: Secured in keys/firmware_aes_256.key (Never exposed to client-side).
